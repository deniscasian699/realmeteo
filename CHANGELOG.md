## [Latest] — September 2026

### Added
- Real-time weather with current temperature and conditions
- 7-day detailed weather forecast
- 24-hour hourly forecast
- Feels-like temperature, humidity, wind speed and direction, UV index
- Visibility, sunrise, sunset, and moon phases
- Rain probability and precipitation data
- Home screen widgets: compact, large, hourly, date & time, and premium
- Favorite cities with quick navigation
- Smart offline weather cache
- Morning weather notifications
- Severe weather alerts (Premium)
- Premium features: 14-day forecast, interactive charts, smart suggestions
- City weather comparison (Premium)
- Customizable Premium widget
- Supporter and Premium Lifetime one-time purchases
- Monthly Supporter and Premium subscriptions
- 7-day free trial for Premium subscription
- Supporter badge, Lifetime badge, and Monthly Supporter badge
- Exclusive app icons for Lifetime and Monthly Supporter
- Romanian and English language support
- Celsius and Fahrenheit toggle
- Light and dark theme support
- Widget themes, transparency, and display options
- GDPR, CCPA, and U.S. state privacy compliance
- RevenueCat integration for purchase management
- BigDataCloud reverse geocoding fallback
- Google AdMob advertising (removable via Supporter tier)
- Wind direction display alongside wind speed
- Date & time home screen widget
- Customizable main-page cards with drag-and-drop ordering and section search
- Live precipitation radar and Open-Meteo forecast model selection
- River discharge and marine-weather cards with location search
- Air quality, soil, cloud-layer, solar, dew-point, elevation, and wind-compass details
- Forecast-model comparison, forecast evolution, and local climate views
- Historical weather views and 12/24-hour clock options

### Changed
- Supporter and Monthly Supporter tiers now remove all advertising permanently
- Premium and Premium Lifetime unlock features but do not remove ads
