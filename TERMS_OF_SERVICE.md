## 1. 📋 The Service

RealMeteo displays weather forecasts, hourly and multi-day outlooks, sunrise
and sunset times, moon phases, home-screen widgets, and optional weather
notifications.

Weather data is supplied by **Open-Meteo.** City names are resolved using your
device's built-in geocoder and, when that is unavailable, the **BigDataCloud**
reverse-geocoding API. We do not generate forecasts ourselves.

The app requires an internet connection to fetch fresh data. Previously
downloaded forecasts remain visible offline, but they will be out of date.

## 2. ⚠️ Accuracy and Safety

Weather forecasts are **inherently uncertain.** They are statistical
predictions produced by numerical models, and they are frequently wrong —
particularly beyond a few days.

> **⚠️ Do not use RealMeteo for safety-critical decisions.**
>
> This includes aviation, maritime navigation, mountaineering, professional
> agriculture, construction planning, or any situation where inaccurate
> weather information could endanger life or property. Always consult your
> official national meteorological service and any severe-weather warnings
> it issues.

The app is provided for general informational purposes only. We make no
warranty that forecasts will be accurate, complete, or timely, and we accept
no liability for decisions made on the basis of the information shown.
